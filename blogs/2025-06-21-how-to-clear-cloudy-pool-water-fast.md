Faulty filters, chemical imbalances, and contamination from swimmers or debris are frequent causes of cloudy pool water. Luckily, you can usually clear clouds from your pool in 24-48 hours. Replacing filters, adjusting chemicals, and flushing contaminants can quickly clear up clouds.
